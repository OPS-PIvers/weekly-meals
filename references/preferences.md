## Effort

The slate must balance effort, not just prep time. Total burden = active prep + babysitting/monitoring + cleanup + coordination.

- **At most one High-effort meal per week.**
- **At least one Low-effort meal per week.**
- Third meal is flexible.

Effort definitions (rough guide):

- **Low** — minimal active prep (~10 min), few ingredients, one pan/sheet, little monitoring. A weeknight-tired meal.
- **Med** — moderate prep (~20 min active), some multitasking, manageable cleanup.
- **High** — long cook, multi-stage, monitoring/coordination, or a new technique. The smoker cooks and "try something new" live here.

> Note: capping *active prep* at ~20 min for non-High meals is a useful heuristic, but judge the whole experience — a 20-min-prep dish that needs constant babysitting is not Low.
